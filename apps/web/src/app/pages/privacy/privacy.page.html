Kebabarium is a non-commercial fan site for rating shawarma and kebab spots.

What we store

  • Reviews you leave: stars, optional text and signature, the spot, the time.
  • Anti-spam markers: a one-way hash of your IP address and of your network (/24 or /64), rotated monthly, and a random device id in a cookie. We never store your IP address itself.
  • If you log in with Google: your Google account id, email, first and last name, profile picture, the nickname you choose and your settings. Your email is never shown to anyone.
  • Cookies: kb_session (keeps you logged in, 30 days) and kb_dev (device id for anti-spam, 1 year). No advertising or tracking cookies.

Third-party content

Spot data, ratings and reviews marked “Google” come from Google Maps. Posts marked “Threads” are public posts found via the Threads API; we show the author handle and a link to the original. The map is provided by Google Maps; Cloudflare Turnstile checks that reviewers are human.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your ratings, hide yourself from the leaderboard and choose whether your real name is shown. To delete your account and all your reviews, write to the address below.
